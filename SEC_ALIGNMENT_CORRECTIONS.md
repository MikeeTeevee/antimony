# SEC Compliance Auditing Questions (30-Question Framework)

## Overview
This document contains 30 auditing questions organized across 5 regulatory compliance areas, each with direct SEC section references.

---

## 1. Governance & Ownership (6 Questions)

### Governance & Ownership

**Q1 - SEC. 302:** Have the principal officers explicitly accepted responsibility for establishing and maintaining internal controls?

**Q2 - SEC. 302:** Can the signing officers confirm they have reviewed the annual or quarterly reports prior to filing?

**Q3 - SEC. 301:** Is the audit committee composed entirely of independent members of the board of directors?

**Q4 - SEC. 406:** Has the company adopted a code of ethics for senior financial officers?

**Q5 - SEC. 906:** Do the CEO and CFO provide a written statement certifying the report fairly presents the financial condition?

**Q6 - SEC. 1105:** Has the company verified that no current officer or director is subject to an SEC bar?

---

## 2. Risk & Fraud Awareness (6 Questions)

### Risk & Fraud Awareness

**Q7 - SEC. 404:** Does management perform an annual assessment of the effectiveness of the internal control structure?

**Q8 - SEC. 302:** Have officers disclosed to auditors any fraud involving management or employees with roles in internal controls?

**Q9 - SEC. 303:** Is there a policy prohibiting the fraudulent influence or misleading of independent accountants?

**Q10 - SEC. 806:** Are whistleblower protections in place to prevent discrimination against employees reporting fraud?

**Q11 - SEC. 1107:** Is the organization aware of the criminal penalties for retaliating against informants?

**Q12 - SEC. 807:** Is the company aware that executing a scheme to defraud shareholders is a specific criminal offense?

---

## 3. Control Activities (6 Questions)

### Control Activities

**Q13 - SEC. 404:** Has management established an adequate internal control structure for financial reporting?

**Q14 - SEC. 402:** Does the company prohibit extending personal loans to directors or executive officers?

**Q15 - SEC. 302:** Are controls designed to ensure material information is made known to signing officers?

**Q16 - SEC. 401:** Do financial reports disclose all material off-balance sheet transactions?

**Q17 - SEC. 306:** Are executives prohibited from trading during pension fund blackout periods?

**Q18 - SEC. 201:** Are controls in place to prevent the external auditor from performing prohibited non-audit services?

---

## 4. Monitoring & Testing (6 Questions)

### Monitoring & Testing

**Q19 - SEC. 302:** Have officers evaluated the effectiveness of internal controls within 90 days prior to the report?

**Q20 - SEC. 302:** Have officers disclosed all significant deficiencies and material weaknesses to the auditors?

**Q21 - SEC. 302:** Does the report indicate if there were significant changes in internal controls subsequent to the evaluation?

**Q22 - SEC. 404:** Has the external auditor attested to management's assessment of internal controls?

**Q23 - SEC. 204:** Does the auditor report critical accounting policies and alternative treatments to the audit committee?

**Q24 - SEC. 301:** Has the audit committee established procedures for the receipt and treatment of complaints?

---

## 5. Documentation & Evidence (5 Questions)

### Documentation & Evidence

**Q25 - SEC. 802:** Are procedures in place to prevent the destruction or alteration of records to impede investigations?

**Q26 - SEC. 103:** Are audit work papers and related information maintained for at least 7 years?

**Q27 - SEC. 802:** Are audit or review workpapers maintained for 5 years from the end of the fiscal period?

**Q28 - SEC. 403:** Are changes in equity ownership filed electronically within two business days?

**Q29 - SEC. 409:** Is the company capable of disclosing material changes to the public on a rapid and current basis?

**Q30 - SEC. 1102:** Is there a prohibition against corruptly tampering with objects or records to impair their integrity?
